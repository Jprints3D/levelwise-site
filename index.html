Privacy Policy Support

Levelwise is a personal tracker for the work you want to keep doing. You add tasks in five categories — Micro, Daily, Weekly, Project, and Life — complete them, and earn XP toward levels, streaks, and a weekly goal. No characters, avatars, or ads.

Your account lives on your device and in your own data on Supabase. Project and Life goals, plus extra styles, are part of Levelwise Pro. Micro, Daily, and Weekly tasks stay free.

The iOS app (and a forthcoming Android app) use the same account. This site hosts the public privacy policy and support pages required by the App Store and Google Play.
