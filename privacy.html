Privacy Policy

Levelwise. Last updated November 12, 2026.

Home Support

Levelwise is a habit and task tracker. It is not an advertising product and it does not sell your data.

Who this covers

This policy applies to the Levelwise iOS app, its widgets, a forthcoming Android app, this website, and the Supabase backend those clients use.

Information we collect

  • Account. Email address and a password you choose, stored by Supabase Auth, so you can sign in on this device and others.
  • Your content. Task titles, notes, categories, schedules, completion history, progress, and settings you save in the app. If you import a calendar event as a task, the title (and the schedule you keep) is stored with your other tasks.
  • Purchases. App Store (and, later, Google Play) transaction identifiers needed to restore Levelwise Pro and paid cosmetics. We do not receive your full card number.

We do not collect precise location, contacts, photos, or browsing history. We do not use your data for tracking or third-party advertising.

Calendar access

If you grant calendar access, the app can show events from calendars you pick and can import an event as a task when you ask it to. Time blocks you create in the app are written only to a dedicated “Levelwise” calendar on the device (an existing “XP Tracker” calendar from earlier versions is renamed in place, not copied). The app does not change events on your other calendars. Calendar data stays on the device unless you import an event, in which case the task content is stored with your account as described above. Synced time blocks may also appear on other devices that sync that calendar (for example, through iCloud).

How we use this information

Only to operate the app: sign you in, sync your tasks and progress, show widgets, and honor purchases. We do not sell personal information and we do not share it for advertising.

Processors

Account, task, and purchase-verification data is stored and processed by Supabase on our behalf (hosted at sjwlpdotpwetyflpeosa.supabase.co). Purchases are processed by Apple (and, later, Google) under their terms. We do not use a separate analytics or advertising SDK.

Retention and deletion

We keep your account and content until you ask us to delete them or you delete your account. You can also remove individual tasks in the app. To request deletion of your account and server-side data, use the contact options on the Support page. Backups may lag a short time after deletion.

Security

Traffic uses HTTPS. Access to your tasks on the server is enforced with row-level security so other users cannot read them. Signing in on a new device requires your password.

Children

Levelwise is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them.

Your choices

You can decline calendar permission and still use the rest of the app. You can sign out, change your password, and request account deletion. Purchase restore uses the store account on the device.

Changes

If this policy changes in a material way, we will update the date above and, when required, notify you in the app or by email.
