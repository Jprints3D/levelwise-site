FAQ

I used to have “XP Tracker.” Where did it go?
Levelwise is the same app under a new name and a new App Store / Play identity. After you install Levelwise, sign in with the same email and password. Then delete the old XP Tracker app if it is still on your Home Screen. Your tasks and progress live on the server, not in the old app. If you had calendar sync on, grant calendar access in Levelwise so it can rename the existing “XP Tracker” calendar to “Levelwise” without duplicating your blocks. You will need to add the widgets again.

I have to sign in again. Did I lose my data?
No. The new app has a new bundle ID, so it cannot read the old keychain session. Sign in with the same account. Progress, tasks, and purchases tied to that account are still there.

How do I delete my account or data?
Use the contact options above. Ask for deletion of your Levelwise account and server-side data, and include the email you sign in with.

What does the app collect?
Email (for sign-in), the tasks and progress you save, and purchase identifiers needed to restore Levelwise Pro or paid cosmetics. Calendar access stays on the device except for events you choose to import as tasks. There is no tracking and no ads. See the Privacy Policy.

Purchases and restore.
Levelwise Pro is $1.99 per month or $9.99 lifetime. Themes such as Sakura, Starfall, and Warp, and the Arcade Chomp bar, can also be bought on their own. Restore purchases while signed in. Cream is included with Pro; it is not a separate paid item.

Widgets are missing after the rename.
Add them again: long-press the Home Screen → + → Levelwise.
